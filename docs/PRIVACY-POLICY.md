# Privacy Policy - Biblical Lessons for Entrepreneurs

**Last Updated: November 2024**

## Overview

Biblical Lessons for Entrepreneurs ("App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information**: Email address, password, and display name (required for account creation)
- **Profile Information**: Any optional profile picture or biography you choose to add
- **Lesson Progress**: Tracking of lessons you've viewed, marked as complete, or favorited
- **Preferences**: Your notification settings, preferred lesson categories, and time preferences
- **Communication**: Any messages or feedback you send us through the app

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information**: Device type, model, operating system version, unique device identifier
- **Usage Information**: Lessons accessed, time spent on lessons, search queries, filtering preferences
- **Location Information**: General location (country/region) from IP address for regional content
- **Analytics**: App crashes, performance metrics, feature usage patterns
- **Push Notification Data**: FCM tokens for delivering push notifications

### 1.3 Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

**Firebase (Google)**
- Authentication service for sign-up/login
- Firestore database for storing your account and progress data
- Cloud Messaging for push notifications
- Analytics for app usage and crash reporting
- [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:
- Creating and managing your user account
- Delivering lessons and educational content
- Tracking your learning progress and statistics
- Sending push notifications for daily lessons and reminders (with your consent)
- Improving app features and user experience
- Diagnosing technical issues and app crashes
- Complying with legal obligations
- Personalizing your learning experience (recommended lessons based on preferences)

## 3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- Secure encryption for sensitive data (passwords, tokens)
- Keychain storage for authentication tokens on your device
- HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
- Firebase security rules to protect your data at rest
- Regular security updates and monitoring

**Important**: No security system is impenetrable. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

### 4.2 Data Sharing Exceptions
We may disclose your information in the following limited circumstances:
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, court order, or government request
- **Service Providers**: To Firebase and other service providers who assist us in operating the app
- **Safety**: To protect against fraud, security threats, or physical harm
- **Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, or bankruptcy

### 4.3 Firebase Data Processing
Firebase processes data as a data processor on our behalf. Your data is subject to Google's Firebase Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 5. Your Rights and Controls

### 5.1 Access and Correction
You have the right to:
- Access your personal information stored in your account
- Correct inaccurate information
- Update your profile and preferences at any time

### 5.2 Data Deletion
- You can delete your account at any time through app settings
- Upon account deletion, your personal information will be removed from our systems
- Some data may be retained if required by law or for legitimate business purposes
- Note: Cached data on your device may need to be cleared separately

### 5.3 Push Notifications
- You can disable push notifications in your device settings
- You can manage notification preferences within the app (Categories, frequency, time)
- You can opt-out at any time without affecting other app features

### 5.4 Location Services
- We do not collect precise location data
- General location is inferred from IP address and can be disabled in device settings

## 6. Children's Privacy

This app is designed for adults (entrepreneurs and business owners). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the child's account.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Account Data**: Retained while your account is active; deleted upon account deletion
- **Progress Data**: Retained as long as you use the app for learning progress tracking
- **Analytics Data**: Retained for up to 90 days for performance analysis
- **Backup Data**: May be retained in backups for up to 30 days after deletion
