Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

If you signed in with Apple, your password is managed through your Apple ID. For email sign-in, tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen to receive a reset link.

How do I enable/disable notifications?

Go to your Profile tab, then tap "Notification Preferences" to customize when and how often you receive lesson reminders.

Can I access lessons offline?

Yes! Lessons you've viewed are cached for offline access. However, you'll need an internet connection to sync your progress across devices.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account and all associated data, go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. This action cannot be undone.

The app isn't loading lessons

Please check your internet connection and try again. If the problem persists, try closing and reopening the app, or contact support.
